# BLE3D Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical claims with evidence.
- Welcome questions and help new contributors find context.
- Respect privacy, especially when sharing radio captures, screenshots, or logs.
- Accept maintainer decisions gracefully and continue disagreements without personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Publishing another person's private information or device identifiers without permission
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated bad-faith argument
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and focused on community safety.

Report conduct concerns privately to a project maintainer. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. Maintainers will protect the privacy of reporters and review concerns as promptly and fairly as possible.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing BLE3D or its community.
